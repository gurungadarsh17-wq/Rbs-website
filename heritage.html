Heritage Sites

Seven UNESCO World Heritage Sites — each a testament to two thousand years of Nepali civilisation.

UNESCO विश्व सम्पदा

World Heritage
of Nepal

Within the Kathmandu Valley alone, seven monuments of exceptional universal value were inscribed by UNESCO in 1979 — an unparalleled concentration of sacred architecture and urban heritage.

UNESCO — Hindu Sacred Site

पशुपतिनाथ

Pashupatinath Temple

The most sacred Hindu temple in Nepal — dedicated to Pashupati, an aspect of Shiva — sits on the banks of the holy Bagmati River. Dating to the 5th century CE, it is one of the subcontinent's most important pilgrimage destinations, with funeral ghats that serve as a constant, moving reminder of life's cycles.

Only Hindus may enter the main temple complex, but from the opposite bank, all can witness the ancient rhythm of prayer, cremation, and river-bathing that has continued unbroken for millennia.

UNESCO — Buddhist Sacred Site

बौद्धनाथ

Boudhanath Stupa

One of the largest stupas in the world, Boudhanath is the heart of Tibetan Buddhist Nepal. Its all-seeing eyes of the Buddha gaze out across a mandala-shaped complex that draws Tibetan pilgrims, monks, and meditation seekers from across Asia.

The evening kora — the circumambulation of the stupa — where hundreds of devotees spin prayer wheels and chant mantras in the amber glow, is one of Nepal's most transcendent experiences.

UNESCO — Ancient Stupa Complex

स्वयम्भूनाथ

Swayambhunath Stupa

Perched atop a hill above Kathmandu, the Swayambhunath complex — lovingly called the Monkey Temple — is over 2,500 years old. The gilded spire, hung with prayer flags, watches over the valley from all directions.

Ancient texts claim the valley was once a great lake, and Swayambhunath rose from a lotus that bloomed at its centre. Pilgrims who climb the 365 steps to its summit are rewarded with panoramic views and a complex of shrines sacred to both Hindus and Buddhists.

Ancient City ललितपुर

Patan Durbar Square

The city of Lalitpur (Patan) preserves the most refined examples of Newari architecture — royal courtyards, pagoda temples, and a living museum of medieval urban life.

Medieval City भक्तपुर

Bhaktapur Durbar Square

The best-preserved of the three Durbar Squares, Bhaktapur feels frozen in the 15th century — its 55-Window Palace, pottery squares, and terracotta-brick streets intact and living.

Sacred Birthplace लुम्बिनी

Lumbini — Birthplace of Buddha

In the southwestern Terai, Lumbini is one of the holiest places in the world — the exact birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama, who became the Buddha, around 563 BCE.
