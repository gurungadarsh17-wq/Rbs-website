This website was created as a cultural and informational platform to introduce Nepal to users in an engaging and visually immersive way.
It aims to showcase Nepal’s identity through its geography, traditions, heritage sites, festivals, and cuisine. The website serves as a digital introduction
to Nepal for international audiences, highlighting what makes the country unique from the Himalayas and sacred temples to its diverse culture and living traditions.
The design follows a modern UI/UX approach to make exploration simple, visually appealing, and interactive, encouraging users to learn more about Nepal
in an intuitive way.
