From the world's highest peak to sacred lakes and ancient valleys — Nepal's geography is extraordinary.

The Roof of the World
सगरमाथा

Mount Everest

At 8,848.86 metres — the definitive highest point on Earth. Known to Nepalis as Sagarmatha (सगरमाथा — "Forehead of the Sky") and to Tibetans as Chomolungma, Everest is Nepal's most iconic landmark and a magnet for climbers, trekkers, and dreamers worldwide.

The Everest Base Camp trek — through Sherpa villages, rhododendron forests, and high-altitude monasteries — is one of the world's great walks, even for those who never intend to summit.

Must-See
Landmarks

Beyond Everest, Nepal's landscapes range from subtropical jungles to high-altitude deserts — each region a world of its own.

Lakeside City पोखरा

Pokhara Valley

A jewel of the Himalayas — Pokhara sits beside the mirror-still Phewa Lake with the Annapurna range rising dramatically behind it. The city is Nepal's adventure capital, the gateway for Annapurna Circuit trekkers and paragliders.

Trekking Circuit अन्नपूर्ण

Annapurna Circuit

One of the world's greatest trekking routes — a 160-200km loop through subtropical forests, terraced rice paddies, high desert plateau, and over the 5,416m Thorong La pass. Every day a different landscape, a different world.

National Park चितवन

Chitwan National Park

Nepal's first national park and a UNESCO World Heritage Site — home to the one-horned rhinoceros, Bengal tiger, gharial crocodile, and over 500 species of birds in the dense terai jungle.

Sacred Lake रारा ताल

Rara Lake

Nepal's largest lake, hidden in the remote northwest Himalaya at 2,990m. Surrounded by pine and juniper forest, its extraordinary sapphire-blue waters — almost entirely unspoiled — reflect the mountains like a mirror.

High Desert Kingdom मुस्ताङ

Upper Mustang

A restricted region that was an independent kingdom until 2008 — Upper Mustang feels like stepping into ancient Tibet. Wind-sculpted ochre cliffs, cave monasteries, and walled mud-brick cities preserved in high-altitude isolation.

Valley Trekking लाङटाङ

Langtang Valley

Just two days north of Kathmandu, Langtang is Nepal's most accessible high-altitude trek — a serene valley of yak pastures, glacial rivers, and Tamang villages with unobstructed views of the Himalayas.
